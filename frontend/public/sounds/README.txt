CUSTOM NOTIFICATION SOUND INSTRUCTIONS:
======================================
To use your own custom MP3 notification sound:

1. Drop your `.mp3` file into this directory:
   frontend/public/sounds/notification.mp3

2. Whenever news or an alert breaks, Equity Arena will automatically play your `notification.mp3` file!

Note: If no MP3 file is placed here, the app smoothly falls back to the built-in Web Audio chime.
